Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get the cleanest signature cutout? ▼

Use the 'Signature / Stamp (High Contrast)' mode. Write your signature on clean white paper under good lighting, snap a photo, and the tool will automatically preserve the dark ink strokes while making the paper completely transparent.

Can I target and remove any specific background color? ▼

Yes! Switch to 'Eyedropper Mode' and click anywhere on the image preview to sample the target RGB color. Then adjust the tolerance slider between 20% and 45% to clean up surrounding gradients and shadows.
